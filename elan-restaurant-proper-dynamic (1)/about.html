ÉLAN was created around a simple belief: vegetarian food deserves the same sense of occasion as any great dining experience.

Our kitchen works with vegetables, grains, dairy, herbs and spices to create food that feels familiar at heart but contemporary on the plate.

We are located opposite Sipna College, Amravati, Maharashtra — a relaxed destination for everyday lunches, dinners and celebrations.
